Privacy and data

Is my spending data private?

Yes. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. SpendCast has no account, no cloud sync and no servers.

Does SpendCast read my SMS or connect to my bank?

No. It never reads your messages and never connects to a bank. You enter expenses yourself, which is why it needs no sensitive permissions.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Open Settings, tap Export / Backup and save the JSON file. On the new phone, install SpendCast and use Import / Restore.

Can I open my data in Excel?

Yes. Settings has Export as CSV, which opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on App PIN Lock in Settings to require a PIN each time SpendCast opens.

Everyday use

Can I track more than one wallet?

Yes. Create separate wallets, for example personal and household, and switch between them from the Dashboard.

How do recurring expenses work?

Mark an expense as recurring and choose how often it repeats. SpendCast adds each new entry for you and shows the next one under Upcoming.

What if I don't know the exact amount?

Enter your best estimate and mark it as approximate. It still counts towards your budget and is flagged in the list.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Pick your currency in Settings. It applies everywhere in the app.

How do budgets work?

Set a monthly limit for a category and the app shows how much is left. The bar turns amber as you get close and red once you pass it.

General

Is SpendCast free?

Yes, SpendCast is free to download from Google Play.

Does it show ads?

No. There are no ads and no third-party tracking.
