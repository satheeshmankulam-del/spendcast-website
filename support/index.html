Back up and move to a new phone

  1. Open Settings and tap Export / Backup. This saves a JSON file with your wallets, expenses, budgets and goals.
  2. Find the file in the Files app, under Internal storage, in Documents or Download.
  3. Copy it to your new phone, for example through Google Drive, email to yourself or a cable.
  4. Install SpendCast, open Settings, tap Import / Restore and choose the file.

Before you uninstall: uninstalling erases everything permanently. Export a backup first.

Open your data in Excel or Google Sheets

Settings has Export as CSV. The file has one row per expense, with the date, title, category and amount, so you can build your own charts or share it with an accountant.

Delete your data

SpendCast has no account and no servers, so your data exists only on your phone.

  1. Open Settings and tap Clear entire data in the Danger zone.
  2. Confirm. All wallets, expenses, budgets and goals are erased.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything. Delete any backup files you exported as well. Read the privacy policy for details.
