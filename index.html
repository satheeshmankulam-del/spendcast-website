What SpendCast does

Dashboard

This month at a glance, for the wallet you pick.

  • Total spent, with the change from last month
  • A monthly budget ring and what's left
  • Spending split by category
  • Income, remaining and used, side by side
Expenses

Every entry, grouped by day.

  • Add an expense in seconds with the quick amount pad
  • Mark an amount as approximate when you don't have the bill
  • Recurring entries for rent, subscriptions and EMIs
  • Filter by category, wallet, date range or amount
Insights

Where the money actually goes.

  • Six-month trend of spending against income
  • Month-by-month comparison
  • Which day of the week you spend most
  • All-time totals by category
Categories and budgets

Limits that tell you before you overspend.

  • Monthly budget for each category
  • Progress bars that turn amber, then red
  • An overall monthly budget cap
  • Add, rename or remove categories
Goals

Save towards something specific.

  • Target amount, deadline and saved-so-far
  • Progress ring and days left
  • As many goals as you need
Wallets, settings and data

Your money, your rules.

  • Separate wallets, such as personal and home
  • Pick your currency
  • Dark mode and an app PIN lock
  • Export a JSON backup or a CSV for Excel and Google Sheets

How to start

  1. Add an expense

    Tap + on the Dashboard, enter the amount and pick a category.

  2. Set your budgets

    Give each category a monthly limit, and an overall cap if you want one.

  3. Record your income

    Add your salary or any other income for the month.

  4. Watch the insights

    After a few weeks, Insights shows your trends and busiest spending days.

Common questions

Is my spending data private?

Yes. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. SpendCast has no account, no cloud sync and no servers.

Does SpendCast read my SMS or connect to my bank?

No. It never reads your messages and never connects to a bank. You enter expenses yourself, which is why it needs no sensitive permissions.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Open Settings, tap Export / Backup and save the JSON file. On the new phone, install SpendCast and use Import / Restore.

Can I open my data in Excel?

Yes. Settings has Export as CSV, which opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
